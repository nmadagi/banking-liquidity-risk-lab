# banking_book.csv

One row per business day, 2023-01-02 to 2026-08-31, 956 rows. USD millions except where noted. Generated by `data/generate.py` with a fixed seed; a test asserts the file matches the generator.

| column | what it is |
|---|---|
| date | business day |
| dep_retail_stable | insured retail deposits with an established relationship |
| dep_retail_less_stable | insured retail deposits that rate-shop or are new |
| dep_small_business | insured small business operating accounts |
| dep_corp_operational | corporate deposits tied to payments, payroll and cash management |
| dep_corp_nonop | corporate excess cash with no operational reason to stay |
| dep_fi_nonop | deposits from other financial institutions |
| loan_commercial, loan_mortgage, loan_consumer | loan books |
| reserves | cash at the central bank |
| treasuries | US government securities (HQLA level 1) |
| agency_mbs | agency mortgage backed securities (level 2A) |
| corp_bonds | investment grade corporate bonds (level 2B) |
| other_assets | premises, intangibles, everything that is not liquid |
| wholesale_short | unsecured wholesale funding maturing inside 30 days |
| wholesale_long | term funding over one year, including any contingency borrowing |
| other_liabilities | accruals and other |
| equity | shareholders' funds |
| und_retail_lines | undrawn retail and small business credit lines |
| und_corp_credit | undrawn credit facilities to non-financial corporates |
| und_corp_liquidity | undrawn liquidity facilities to non-financial corporates |
| und_fi_facilities | undrawn facilities to financial institutions |
| rate_* | the deposit rate paid to each segment, percent |
| stress_index | 0 to 1, the planted stress path (a real bank would build this from market and idiosyncratic indicators) |
| fed_funds | policy rate, percent |

`events.csv` lists the two stress episodes and the one business initiative with their dates.

Typical levels: total deposits 82,000 to 95,000; loans 82,000 to 96,000; HQLA 24,000 to 40,000. The balance sheet identity holds every day: reserves are the plug.
